Lung Cancer Analytics Platform
Project Overview
This dashboard was developed to provide a comprehensive view of the lung cancer patient journey, starting from patient demographics and clinical characteristics to biomarker testing, treatment patterns, and treatment outcomes.
The objective was to create a centralized analytical platform that helps stakeholders understand:
	• Patient population characteristics
	• Disease stage and subtype distribution
	• Biomarker testing and positivity rates
	• Treatment utilization patterns
	• Treatment effectiveness and response outcomes

📄 Page 1: Executive Overview
Business Objective
Provide a high-level summary of the overall lung cancer patient population and treatment outcomes.
Key Insights
This page answers:
	• How many patients are included in the study?
	• What is the average patient age?
	• What percentage of patients underwent biomarker testing?
	• What are the overall response and disease control rates?
	• How patient registrations are trending over time?
	• How patients are distributed across geographic regions?
KPIs
	• Total Patients
	• Average Patient Age
	• Biomarker Coverage %
	• Overall Response Rate (ORR)
	• Disease Control Rate (DCR)
Visuals
	• Monthly Patient Trend
	• Patients by State / Zone
	• Treatment Line Distribution
	• Tumor Subtype Overview
